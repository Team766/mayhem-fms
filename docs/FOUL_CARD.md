# Medieval Mayhem Foul Card

A one-page summary for referees. The gists are short reminders, not the rules: the full text is in the game manual,
section 4.5, and in [specs/RULES.md](../specs/RULES.md). A foul's points go to the **other** alliance.

## Fouls that also give the other alliance a ranking point

Pick the rule on the referee page, or the ranking point isn't given.

| Code | Major | Gist | Other alliance gets |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MA2601** | 10 | Touching the opponent's balance beam in endgame | Endgame RP |
| **MA2602** | 10 | Touching an opponent robot on its balance beam in endgame | Endgame RP |
| **MA2603** | 10 | Entering the opponent's safe house or safe zone in auton | Auton RP |

## Minor fouls: 5 points

| Code | Gist |
|---|---|
| G403 | Crossing the midfield line in auton |
| G423 | Damaging or impairing an opponent robot (also major) |
| G425 | Pinning a robot more than 3 seconds |
| MA2606 | In the opponent's loading zone, safe house or safe zone, or touching the beam before endgame (every 10 s) |
| MA2607 | Treasure entered other than through the loading holes (not the toss) |
| MA2608 | Thrown treasure bounces first off anything but your robot or loading zone floor |
| MA2609 | Repeated or unsafe contact with your own shelf |
| MA2610 | Starting auton with more than 1 piece (no shelf points in auton) |
| MA2611 | Descoring the opponent's pieces |
| MA2612 | Deliberately destroying a treasure |
| MA2613 | Adding treasures in endgame (they don't score) |
| MA2614 | Placing or shooting treasures in the opponent's zones |
| MA2615 | Not obeying the referee area signs (also major) |
| MA2616 | Controlling more than 2 game pieces (extras don't score) |
| MA2617 | Throwing onto the shelf from outside your safe house (doesn't score) |
